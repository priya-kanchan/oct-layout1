Project Overview

A calm, lake-side address
built around you

Aqua Vista Residences is a landmark development of 4 towers rising 32 floors, facing the serene Varthur lake. Each home is designed for natural light and cross-ventilation, with wide sundecks that bring the water views indoors.

Because only 4 homes share each floor, you get privacy, three sides open and no common walls with the lift lobby.
